Setup & booking

Allow 30–60 minutes before the event for setup. The cart measures 1.3m × 0.7m and needs a clear access route. Without direct lift access, a S$30–50 surcharge applies. Hot stations may require power.

We recommend 3–4 oysters per guest. To secure the booking, confirm within 14 days with a deposit and signed service agreement. Full payment is due before the event. Reply to the menu email and our team will help with the details.
